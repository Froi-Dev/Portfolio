# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Recruiters and hiring teams evaluating Froilan De Vera’s development work, technical range, and credentials.
- Prospective clients looking for related work and a way to discuss a project.
- Developers exploring how a project works, which tools it uses, and where its source is available.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio introduces Froilan as a full-stack developer and makes his web, Windows, and Android projects easy to evaluate. Visitors can move from a project preview to its capabilities and stack, download the CV, open credentials, or contact him.

## Positioning

Froilan uses AI-assisted workflows alongside full-stack development and cybersecurity studies. The portfolio grounds that description in the applications he has built and the evidence supplied for them.

## Operating Context

One light page covers the introduction, a quick About, projects, technology stack, credentials, and contact. The project catalog groups work by application type. The Christmas edition is available at its archive URL and starts with animation and audio paused.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Project entries show the supplied screenshots, original descriptions, and feature lists. Live demos and repositories appear where their addresses are available.
- Credentials can be previewed and opened from their supplied files. Verification links appear where provided.
- The contact form prepares a draft in the visitor’s email app. The email address and GitHub profile are also available directly.
- The CV, images, and credential documents are local assets.

## Brand Commitments

Use Froilan De Vera’s full name, supplied portrait, and first-person voice. The tone is editorial and technical. Let concrete product descriptions and screenshots explain the work.

## Evidence on Hand

- `src/data/portfolio.js` and `src/data/projectMedia.js` hold the project descriptions, features, image metadata, and available project links.
- `public/images/projects/` holds the original project banners and optimized copies. `public/images/froilan-portrait.webp` derives from the supplied portrait.
- `src/data/credentials.js`, `public/certificates/`, and `public/documents/Froilan-De-Vera-CV.pdf` hold credential metadata, the original documents, and the CV.

## Product Principles

- Put representative work within one action of the introduction.
- Give each project enough detail for a visitor to understand its purpose and available features.
- Use supplied evidence for claims, verification, and source links.
- Make the contact action explicit about what the visitor needs to do next.
