# Portfolio

## Mode

Experience

## Audience and job

Recruiters assess the work and credentials, prospective clients look for relevant projects, and developers look for implementation details and source. They can scroll the page or jump to a section from the floating list of large, straight, bold links or the mobile Menu.

## Direction contract

**THESIS** — Introduce Froilan as a software developer in a typographic portrait, then let visitors inspect the work directly.

**OWN-WORLD** — Use the supplied reference’s warm paper, black ink, condensed display type, and offset portrait print. The fixed light palette uses the supplied royal blue `#0504aa` for Know Me Better and other primary buttons.

**STORY** — The name, role, and photograph establish whose work this is. Know Me Better jumps to About, which introduces Froilan’s current work, what he wants to build, and his GitHub activity. In Projects, arrows or a swipe move the layered prints through a category and the index opens individual projects. The stack, certificate folders, CV, and contact support the visitor’s next decision.

**FIRST VIEWPORT** — Set the role across the top in condensed display type. Place the name and CV at left, the slightly rotated portrait in the center, and the compact Know Me Better button with a separate downward arrow beside the introduction at right. On phones, center the two-line title and tilted portrait and let the hero fit its content.

**FORM** — A fixed list of large, straight, bold links stays vertically centered at desktop widths, with a ruled drawer on smaller screens. The active link is royal blue with an animated rule. Section links connect the portrait, About, project showcase, stack, Certificates, and contact. Page scroll draws the section rules and updates the top progress line.

**FINISH** — unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

Use supplied work and credential evidence. Keep project details shareable by their anchors, preserve keyboard dismissal and focus return, and honor reduced motion. Describe contact submission as opening an email draft. Keep the seasonal edition available at its archive URL.
